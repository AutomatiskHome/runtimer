Privacy Policy for RunTimer

Last updated: November 19, 2026

At a glance

  • RunTimer has no server of its own. Your workout and health data are never sent to the developer.
  • There are no analytics or tracking services and no advertising SDKs. Uploads to Intervals.icu and Tredict are optional and off by default. The Tredict screen includes a labelled referral link (advertising, see section 4).
  • Unless you choose to share files or enable uploads to Intervals.icu or Tredict, your workout data stays on your devices and in Apple Health. You decide separately for each service whether workouts are sent.

2. What data the app uses and why

RunTimer is an interval timer for run-walk training on iPhone and Apple Watch. The following data is recorded and displayed on your devices. The app requires your system permissions to access protected health, location and motion data. Additional transfers to Intervals.icu or Tredict take place only if you set up the respective connection and enable automatic uploads (see sections 3 and 4).

Apple Health (HealthKit) – on Apple Watch

  • Read and write: heart rate, active energy (calories), walking and running distance, workouts and workout routes.
  • Purpose: showing live values during your workout (heart rate, pace, distance), target-zone alerts, and saving the workout to Apple Health so it appears in the Fitness app.
  • RunTimer uses health data for workout features, not for advertising or tracking. If you enable uploads to Intervals.icu or Tredict or share a workout file yourself, the health data it contains may be sent to the service you choose.

Location (GPS) – on Apple Watch, only during a workout

  • Purpose: recording your route, distance and pace, and detecting when you stop for the automatic pause.
  • The route is saved to Apple Health as part of the workout.

Motion & Fitness – on Apple Watch

  • Purpose: step detection and motion sensors to detect when you stop, for the automatic pause. This data is not stored.

Settings and templates

  • Your interval settings, custom templates and announcement options are stored locally in the app.

Transfer between iPhone and Apple Watch

  • Settings, templates, the live workout status, saved workout files and, if enabled, the diagnostic log are transferred via Apple's system interface "WatchConnectivity" directly between your own paired devices – never to the developer.

Diagnostic log (optional, can be turned off)

  • For testing, the watch can record a log during the workout with timestamps, motion and speed values, phase changes and announcements. It contains no location coordinates.
  • The log is only transferred to and stored on your iPhone. It leaves your devices only if you share it yourself (e.g. by email).
  • You can turn the recording off at any time under Ansagen & Signale → Diagnose (Announcements & Signals → Diagnostics).

Workout files (TCX export)

  • After each saved workout, the watch creates a workout file in TCX format containing time, distance, heart rate, calories, available elevation and GPS data, and the individual workout phases. It is first transferred to your own iPhone and stored there under Trainingsdateien (Workout files).
  • The file leaves your devices if you share it yourself – for example with a training platform such as Runalyze or Strava – or enable automatic uploads to Intervals.icu or Tredict. Processing on each platform is subject to its privacy policy.
  • You can delete the files in the app at any time. A background transfer that has already been scheduled uses its own file copy; deleting the original file does not stop that transfer. To stop further uploads, switch off automatic uploads. This does not delete workouts already stored on a platform.

3. Optional connection to Intervals.icu

RunTimer offers an optional connection to the training service Intervals.icu. Availability depends on whether the connection has been enabled in your app version. You can use the other workout features without this connection. RunTimer does not operate a server for the transfer; data is sent directly from your iPhone to Intervals.icu over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Sign-in and permission

  • Under Verbindungen → Intervals.icu → Mit Intervals.icu verbinden (Connect to Intervals.icu), Apple's web authentication opens the service's sign-in page. You enter your credentials directly with Intervals.icu. RunTimer receives and stores no password.
  • Sign-in uses OAuth with PKCE. RunTimer requests activity permission (ACTIVITY:WRITE) to upload saved workouts. At Intervals.icu, this permission also includes read access to activities; RunTimer currently uses the connection for uploads and does not request calendar or wellness permissions.
  • Intervals.icu returns an access token, your athlete ID and your account name. RunTimer stores these details in the iPhone's local Keychain to display the connected account and authorize subsequent uploads. They are not sent to the watch or the developer.

Automatic uploads and data transferred

  • Automatic uploads are off by default. Signing in alone does not enable them. Only after you turn on Trainings automatisch hochladen (Automatically upload workouts) will workouts started and saved from that point onwards be uploaded. Existing workouts are not automatically uploaded retrospectively. Switching the option off and back on sets a new activation time.
  • The TCX file contains the workout start time and timestamps, duration, distance, heart rate, calories, interval laps, and available GPS coordinates and elevation values. A workout identifier derived from the file contents and the access token are also sent to associate the upload with your account. When a connection is established, Intervals.icu also receives technically necessary connection data such as your IP address.
  • Transfers start after the workout file reaches your iPhone. They may run in the background and use mobile data. Failed uploads may be retried automatically. For this purpose, RunTimer locally stores activation details, the account association, file identifiers, and success and retry status. A temporary file copy is created for background uploads and removed when the respective transfer attempt finishes.

Switching off, disconnecting and deleting

  • You can switch off automatic uploads at any time. RunTimer then stops scheduling further uploads and cancels running or waiting transfers. Data already sent cannot be retrieved by doing this.
  • Konto trennen (Disconnect account) switches uploads off, revokes access at Intervals.icu and, on successful completion, removes the token and account details from the Keychain. If revocation is not possible, for example because there is no internet connection, uploads remain off and the account connection is kept so you can retry disconnecting. You can also revoke access directly in your Intervals.icu account.
  • Switching uploads off, disconnecting, deleting a local workout file or removing RunTimer does not delete activities already stored at Intervals.icu. You manage and delete those activities at Intervals.icu. Workouts stored separately in Apple Health likewise remain until you delete them there.

Intervals.icu is operated by intervals.icu Ltd. Processing by the service, its retention periods, service providers and possible international data transfers are covered by the Intervals.icu Privacy Policy. Its privacy and cookie rules also apply to its sign-in page.

The developer receives no workout data or access tokens through this connection. RunTimer contains no analytics tools or advertising SDKs. See section 4 for the Tredict referral link.

4. Optional connection to Tredict

Under Verbindungen → Tredict (Connections → Tredict), you can enter a personal API token created for RunTimer in your Tredict settings with the activityWrite permission. This connection does not use OAuth and requires neither your Tredict password nor additional read or health-data permissions. The token is stored in this iPhone’s local Keychain, is not sent to the watch or developer, and is checked by Tredict during the next upload.

  • Automatic uploads are a separate option and off by default. Saving a token does not enable them. Once enabled, workouts started and saved from that point onwards are transferred after their TCX file reaches the iPhone. Re-enabling sets a new activation time; replacing a token switches uploads off.
  • Transfers go directly to Tredict over HTTPS, without a developer-operated server. They include the token for authorization and the TCX file with workout times, duration, distance, heart rate, calories, interval laps, and available GPS and elevation data. Tredict also receives technically necessary connection data such as your IP address.
  • Uploads may run in the background, use mobile data, and be retried after temporary failures. RunTimer locally stores the activation time, a local connection identifier, file identifiers and transfer status. Protected temporary copies used for background uploads are removed after each transfer attempt.
  • Personal tokens require a manual visit/login to Tredict at least every ten days to remain active. Uploads require write access in your Tredict account. RunTimer pauses transfers if authorization, permissions or write access are missing. After resolving the issue, choose Erneut versuchen (Retry) to resume.
  • Switching uploads off stops new uploads and cancels waiting or running transfers. Token entfernen (Remove token) deletes the token from this iPhone. This does not revoke it at Tredict: remove it in your Tredict settings as well to revoke it. Data already sent cannot be retrieved; manage and delete uploaded workouts at Tredict. Deleting the app or local files does not delete these workouts.

Processing by Tredict is covered by the Tredict Privacy Policy, including information about its controller, retention and deletion.

Referral link (advertising)

The write-access information in RunTimer includes a labelled developer referral link to Tredict. Only when you open it is the Tredict website requested with the fixed referral code r=tawF9k823. This allows Tredict to attribute the visit to the referral; it also receives your browser’s usual connection data. The link contains no workout data or API token, and the referral code is not attached to uploads. Tredict’s privacy and cookie rules apply to the visit. The logo is bundled with the app and is not downloaded from Tredict.

This information is used only to improve the app. Details are governed by Apple's TestFlight terms.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. You can view and delete locally stored workouts in RunTimer or the Health app and revoke system permissions under Settings → Privacy & Security. You can withdraw consent to optional transfers at any time for the future by switching uploads off or disconnecting your account; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing that has already taken place. You can exercise rights concerning data stored at Intervals.icu or Tredict with that service. Deleting RunTimer does not remove data from Intervals.icu, Tredict or Apple Health. For questions, contact me at the email address above.

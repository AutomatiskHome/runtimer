Privacy Policy for RunTimer

Last updated: November 14, 2026

At a glance

  • RunTimer has no server of its own. Your workout and health data are never sent to the developer.
  • There are no ads, no analytics or tracking services, and no third-party services built in.
  • All data stays on your iPhone, your Apple Watch and in Apple Health – under your control.

1. Controller

Christoph Niesig
Email: runtimerapp@tuta.com

2. What data the app uses and why

RunTimer is an interval timer for run-walk training on iPhone and Apple Watch. The following data is processed only locally on your devices and only after you grant access in the system settings:

Apple Health (HealthKit) – on Apple Watch

  • Read and write: heart rate, active energy (calories), walking and running distance, workouts and workout routes.
  • Purpose: showing live values during your workout (heart rate, pace, distance), target-zone alerts, and saving the workout to Apple Health so it appears in the Fitness app.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or any other purpose and is never shared with third parties.

Location (GPS) – on Apple Watch, only during a workout

  • Purpose: recording your route, distance and pace, and detecting when you stop for the automatic pause.
  • The route is saved to Apple Health as part of the workout.

Motion & Fitness – on Apple Watch

  • Purpose: step detection and motion sensors to detect when you stop, for the automatic pause. This data is not stored.

Settings and templates

  • Your interval settings, custom templates and announcement options are stored locally in the app.

Transfer between iPhone and Apple Watch

  • Settings, templates, the live workout status and, if enabled, the diagnostic log are transferred via Apple's system interface "WatchConnectivity" directly between your own paired devices – never to the developer.

Diagnostic log (optional, can be turned off)

  • For testing, the watch can record a log during the workout with timestamps, motion and speed values, phase changes and announcements. It contains no location coordinates.
  • The log is only transferred to and stored on your iPhone. It leaves your devices only if you share it yourself (e.g. by email).
  • You can turn the recording off at any time under Ansagen & Signale → Diagnose (Announcements & Signals → Diagnostics).

3. No sharing with the developer or third parties

The developer does not receive any personal data through the app. No data is transmitted to servers of the developer or to third parties. The app contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency.

4. Apple services

The app uses features of the operating system (Apple Health, Location Services, motion sensors, WatchConnectivity). Whether and how Apple syncs this data between your devices, e.g. via iCloud, depends on your Apple settings and on Apple's Privacy Policy.

TestFlight (beta testing): If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple provides the developer with certain information, such as your name or email address from the invitation, device and system information, crash reports, and feedback and screenshots you choose to send via TestFlight. This information is used only to improve the app. Details are governed by Apple's TestFlight terms.

5. Contact by email

If you send me an email, I process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). The message is deleted once your request has been handled and no retention obligations apply. The mailbox is hosted by Tuta (Tutao GmbH, Hanover, Germany).

6. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and ensure security. I do not use cookies or analytics tools myself. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since the app does not transfer your data to me, it resides solely on your devices: you can view and delete it at any time in the Health app, revoke permissions under Settings → Privacy & Security, or delete the app. For questions, contact me at the email address above.

8. Changes

If the app changes – for example, if an optional upload of workouts to training platforms is added in the future – this privacy policy will be updated beforehand. The version published here applies.

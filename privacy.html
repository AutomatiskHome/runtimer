Privacy Policy for RunTimer

Last updated: November 20, 2026

At a glance

  • RunTimer has no server of its own. Your workout and health data are never sent to the developer.
  • There are no analytics or tracking services and no advertising SDKs. Uploads to Intervals.icu, Tredict and Runalyze are optional and off by default. The Tredict screen includes a labelled referral link (advertising, see section 3.3).
  • Unless you choose to share files or enable uploads to Intervals.icu, Tredict or Runalyze, your workout data stays on your devices and in Apple Health. You decide separately for each service whether workouts are sent.

2. What data the app uses and why

RunTimer is an interval timer for run-walk training on iPhone and Apple Watch. The following data is recorded and displayed on your devices. The app requires your system permissions to access protected health, location and motion data. Additional transfers to Intervals.icu, Tredict or Runalyze take place only if you set up the respective connection and enable automatic uploads (see section 3).

Apple Health (HealthKit) – on Apple Watch

  • Read and write: heart rate, active energy (calories), walking and running distance, workouts and workout routes.
  • Purpose: showing live values during your workout (heart rate, pace, distance), target-zone alerts, and saving the workout to Apple Health so it appears in the Fitness app.
  • RunTimer uses health data for workout features, not for advertising or tracking. If you enable uploads to Intervals.icu, Tredict or Runalyze or share a workout file yourself, the health data it contains may be sent to the service you choose.

Location (GPS) – on Apple Watch, only during a workout

  • Purpose: recording your route, distance and pace, and detecting when you stop for the automatic pause.
  • The route is saved to Apple Health as part of the workout.

Motion & Fitness – on Apple Watch

  • Purpose: step detection and motion sensors to detect when you stop, for the automatic pause. This data is not stored.

Settings and templates

  • Your interval settings, custom templates and announcement options are stored locally in the app.

Transfer between iPhone and Apple Watch

  • Settings, templates, the live workout status, saved workout files and, if enabled, the diagnostic log are transferred via Apple's system interface "WatchConnectivity" directly between your own paired devices – never to the developer.

Diagnostic log (optional, can be turned off)

  • For testing, the watch can record a log during the workout with timestamps, motion and speed values, phase changes and announcements. It contains no location coordinates.
  • The log is only transferred to and stored on your iPhone. It leaves your devices only if you share it yourself (e.g. by email).
  • You can turn the recording off at any time under Ansagen & Signale → Diagnose (Announcements & Signals → Diagnostics).

Workout files (TCX export)

  • After each saved workout, the watch creates a workout file in TCX format containing time, distance, heart rate, calories, available elevation and GPS data, and the individual workout phases. It is first transferred to your own iPhone and stored there under Trainingsdateien (Workout files).
  • The file leaves your devices if you share it yourself – for example with a training platform such as Runalyze or Strava – or enable automatic uploads to Intervals.icu, Tredict or Runalyze. Processing on each platform is subject to its privacy policy.
  • You can delete the files in the app at any time. A background transfer that has already been scheduled uses its own file copy; deleting the original file does not stop that transfer. To stop further uploads, switch off automatic uploads. This does not delete workouts already stored on a platform.

3. External connections (optional)

At your request, RunTimer can transfer saved workouts to external training platforms. Currently available are Intervals.icu, Tredict and Runalyze. Each connection is optional and set up individually; you can use all other features without them. If further services are added, this section will be updated beforehand.

3.1 Common principles for all connections

  • Off by default: Connecting an account or saving a token does not enable uploads. Only when you switch on Trainings automatisch hochladen (Upload workouts automatically) for a service are workouts started and saved from that point onwards transferred once their TCX file reaches the iPhone. Existing workouts are not uploaded retroactively. Switching off and on again sets a new activation time; changing the account or token switches uploads off.
  • Direct and encrypted: Data is sent from the iPhone directly to the respective service over HTTPS. RunTimer does not operate a server for this; the developer receives no workout data and no credentials through these connections.
  • Data transferred: the TCX file with the workout start time and timestamps, duration, distance, heart rate, calories, interval laps, and available GPS coordinates and elevation values, plus the credentials (token) to associate the upload with your account. When a connection is established, the service also receives technically necessary connection data such as your IP address.
  • Credentials: RunTimer neither needs nor stores any password. Tokens and account details are stored only in this iPhone’s local Keychain and are not shared with the watch or the developer.
  • Background and retries: Uploads may run in the background, use mobile data and be retried automatically after temporary failures. For this, RunTimer locally stores the activation time, an account or connection identifier, file identifiers, and success and retry status. A protected temporary copy of the file is created for background uploads and removed after each transfer attempt.
  • Switching off: You can switch automatic uploads off at any time. RunTimer then schedules no further uploads and cancels waiting or running transfers. Data already sent cannot be retrieved.
  • Data at the service: Switching off, disconnecting, removing a token, deleting a local workout file or removing RunTimer does not delete workouts already stored at the service. You manage and delete those at the respective service. Workouts stored separately in Apple Health likewise remain until you delete them there.
  • Responsibility of the service: Processing by each service – retention periods, service providers, possible international data transfers and deletion – is governed by its privacy policy (linked in the following sections).

3.2 Intervals.icu

  • Operator: intervals.icu Ltd. Availability depends on whether the connection has been enabled in your app version.
  • Sign-in: Under Verbindungen → Intervals.icu → Mit Intervals.icu verbinden (Connections → Intervals.icu → Connect to Intervals.icu), Apple’s web authentication opens the service’s sign-in page. You enter your credentials directly with Intervals.icu. Sign-in uses OAuth with PKCE.
  • Permission: RunTimer requests activity permission (ACTIVITY:WRITE) to upload saved workouts. At Intervals.icu, this permission also includes read access to activities; RunTimer currently uses the connection for uploads and does not request calendar or wellness permissions.
  • Stored details: Intervals.icu returns an access token, your athlete ID and your account name. RunTimer stores them in the Keychain to display the connected account and authorize uploads. Each upload additionally includes a workout identifier derived from the file contents so that a workout is not created twice.
  • Signing in again: If the sign-in expires and you sign in again with the same account, RunTimer resumes uploads with the previous activation time, so workouts saved in the meantime are caught up. For a different account, uploads stay off.
  • Disconnecting: Konto trennen (Disconnect account) switches uploads off, revokes access at Intervals.icu and, on successful completion, removes the token and account details from the Keychain. If revocation is not possible, for example because there is no internet connection, uploads remain off and the account connection is kept so you can retry disconnecting. You can also revoke access directly in your Intervals.icu account.

The Intervals.icu Privacy Policy applies. Its privacy and cookie rules also apply to its sign-in page.

3.3 Tredict

  • Token: Under Verbindungen → Tredict (Connections → Tredict), you can enter a personal API token created for RunTimer in your Tredict settings with the activityWrite permission. This connection does not use OAuth and requires no additional read or health-data permissions. Tredict checks the token during the next upload.
  • Validity and write access: Personal tokens require a manual visit/login to Tredict at least every ten days to remain active. Uploads require write access in your Tredict account. RunTimer pauses transfers if authorization, permissions or write access are missing; after resolving the issue, choose Erneut versuchen (Retry) to resume.
  • Removing the token: Token entfernen (Remove token) deletes the token from this iPhone. This does not revoke it at Tredict: remove it in your Tredict settings as well to revoke it.

The Tredict Privacy Policy applies, including information about its controller, retention and deletion.

Referral link (advertising): The write-access information in RunTimer includes a labelled developer referral link to Tredict. Only when you open it is the Tredict website requested with the fixed referral code r=tawF9k823. This allows Tredict to attribute the visit to the referral; it also receives your browser’s usual connection data. The link contains no workout data or API token, and the referral code is not attached to uploads. Tredict’s privacy and cookie rules apply to the visit. The logo is bundled with the app and is not downloaded from Tredict.

3.4 Runalyze

  • Operator: Runalyze GmbH.
  • Token: Under Verbindungen → Runalyze (Connections → Runalyze), you can enter a personal API token created in your Runalyze settings with an expiry date and the activity_push permission for uploading activities. RunTimer does not use OAuth for this connection.
  • Import status: Runalyze processes files after receiving them. RunTimer checks the import status using the returned upload identifier and stores this identifier locally. Status checks and rate-limited uploads may be retried automatically. If a transfer is uncertain, check whether the workout already exists in Runalyze before retrying manually.
  • Validity: Tokens expire on the selected date and must be replaced. Transfers pause when access is denied.
  • Removing the token: Token entfernen (Remove token) deletes the token from this iPhone. Revoke it in your Runalyze settings as well.

The Runalyze Privacy Policy applies, including when you visit the linked token settings.

4. Apple services

The app uses features of the operating system (Apple Health, Location Services, motion sensors, WatchConnectivity). Whether and how Apple syncs this data between your devices, e.g. via iCloud, depends on your Apple settings and on Apple's Privacy Policy.

TestFlight (beta testing): If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple provides the developer with certain information, such as your name or email address from the invitation, device and system information, crash reports, and feedback and screenshots you choose to send via TestFlight. This information is used only to improve the app. Details are governed by Apple's TestFlight terms.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. You can view and delete locally stored workouts in RunTimer or the Health app and revoke system permissions under Settings → Privacy & Security. You can withdraw consent to optional transfers at any time for the future by switching uploads off or disconnecting your account; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing that has already taken place. You can exercise rights concerning data stored at Intervals.icu, Tredict or Runalyze with that service. Deleting RunTimer does not remove data from Intervals.icu, Tredict, Runalyze or Apple Health. For questions, contact me at the email address above.
